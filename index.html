DFDS: Color-Aware Monocular Defocus Vehicle Distance Estimation with AI Segmentation and Physical Optics

Ongoing research · 2026 National University–Industry Innovation and Implementation Competition

An extension of prior DFDS research that combines Segment Anything, a back-focused single-image setup, CIECAM02 perceptual sharpness, and optimized color-channel weighting for interpretable vehicle distance estimation across different vehicle colors in CARLA.

Current results: RMSE ≤ 0.38 m for red, green, and blue vehicles; 13.44 FPS on an RTX 5080.
